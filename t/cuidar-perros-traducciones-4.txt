=== Language: zu ===
App name: Nakekela Izinja: Virtual Pet
Short description: Imidlalo yezinja: nakekela isilwane sakho, hamba nayo, ubalekele inja embi
Full description:
Uyayithanda imidlalo yezinja nemidlalo yezilwane ezifuywayo? Ku-Nakekela Izinja uthatha inja encane enhle uyenze eyakho bese uyinakekela nsuku zonke: i-simulator yesilwane esifuywayo se-virtual enendaba, ukuhamba, imidlalo emincane ne-adventure.

NAKEKELA INJA YAKHO
Yiphe ukudla namanzi, yigeze, dlala nayo futhi uyise kudokotela wezilwane uma ilimala. Nsuku zonke isilwane sakho sidinga okuthile okusha. Ukunakekela izilwane akukaze kumnandi kangaka.

UKUHAMBA EDOLOBHENI
Yifake intambo bese niphuma niyohamba endaweni. Wela umgwaqo uma irobhothi liluhlaza, yigqokise ngokwesimo sezulu (imvula, iqhwa noma ukushisa) futhi uphathe esikhwameni sakho konke ozokudinga.

IMIDLALO EMINCANE YEZINJA
Usuku ngalunye luhlukile: ukudonsa intambo, i-frisbee, amaqhinga, i-maze, ukuwela umfudlana, imijaho nesitolo esiyimfihlakalo.

BALEKELA INJA EMBI
Inja embi izulazula ikhululekile edolobheni. Hlola ukusabela kwakho ngokushesha ukuze ubaleke ngesikhathi.

UTHANDO NOMSHADO WEZINJA
Inja yakho ihlangana noCongo. Hamba nabo osukwini lwabo lokuqomisana futhi nigubhe umshado wezinja.

GQOKISA ISILWANE SAKHO
Izingubo, izigqoko nezinto zokuhlobisa ezivela ku-Pet Shop ukuze inja yakho ye-virtual ihluke.

KUNGANI UZOWUTHANDA
- Umdlalo wezilwane ezifuywayo we-3D onemifanekiso emibalabala nezilawuli ezilula.
- Umdlalo wezinja ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi: dlala ungaxhunyiwe noma kuphi.
- Ingxube ye-simulator yesilwane esifuywayo se-virtual, i-adventure nemidlalo emincane.
- NgesiSpanishi, ngesiNgisi nangesiPutukezi.

Uma ufuna imidlalo yezinja, imidlalo yokunakekela izilwane ezifuywayo noma i-simulator yenja ye-virtual, lona ngumdlalo wakho! Wulande futhi ube ngumnikazi ongcono kakhulu wenja yakho encane.
