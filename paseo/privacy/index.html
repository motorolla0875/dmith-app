Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

What the game stores. Everything stays on your own phone: your pet's name, your progress, coins, clothes, language and sound settings. The game has no accounts, does not ask for your email or phone number, and does not send your progress to any server of ours.

Age. The first time, we ask the year you were born, only to set up the game for you. It stays on your phone. Players under 13 are not shown personalized ads, are not asked for tracking permission, and purchases require a grown-up to confirm.

Ads. The game shows Unity Ads: optional rewarded videos and, for players 13 and older, an occasional ad between days. Unity may use your phone's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads only if you allow it in the iPhone tracking prompt; you can change it anytime in Settings > Privacy > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Google settings. You can remove ads between days with the "Remove ads" purchase.

Purchases. Purchases are processed by your phone's store (App Store or Google Play). We never see or store your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage them, which receives an anonymous identifier and the purchase details, nothing else.

Deleting your data. Since everything stays on your phone, uninstalling the game deletes everything. If you want us to delete anything related to your purchases or have any questions, write to us.
